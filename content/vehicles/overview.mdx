## Overview

The trailers show a wide mix of road vehicles, along with boats and airboats. A full official vehicle list has not been published, so this page will grow as Rockstar reveals more.

## Confirmed in trailers

- Sports cars and muscle cars
- Motorbikes and quad bikes
- Boats and airboats

## Why they matter

Vehicles are part of how Jason and Lucia carry their gear. Wikipedia reports that the limited weapons loadout is tied to the player's vehicle, which keeps cars and bikes close to the action. Rockstar also built a dedicated team for vehicles, alongside one for NPCs.
