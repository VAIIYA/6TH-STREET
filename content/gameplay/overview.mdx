## Overview

Grand Theft Auto VI is a single-player open-world action game set in the state of Leonida. Players follow two protagonists, Jason and Lucia, from a third-person view.

## What you can do

Players can fight NPCs, steal vehicles, rob establishments and get into high-speed police chases. Wikipedia lists skydiving, wrestling, basketball, exercising, kayaking and scuba diving among the activities available in free roam. The game also has a Snapmatic social media app, returning from Grand Theft Auto V, which parodies 2020s social media.

## Characters and reputation

- Jason and Lucia can be split up or kept together in free roam, and how close they are is optional.
- Both can gain or lose weight, and staying awake for days becomes visible on their faces.
- A Criminal Profile rating tracks each character's reputation, in the spirit of the honour system in Red Dead Redemption 2.
- NPCs react to the player. Rockstar says the game pushes NPC interaction further than Red Dead Redemption 2.

## Weapons

Wikipedia reports that the player carries two primary firearms and two sidearms at a time instead of an unlimited arsenal. Weapons are stored and transported in the player's vehicle.

## Music

The game has radio stations across pop, country, hip-hop, Latin, classic rock, metal and more, with returning stations such as V-Rock, Flash FM and Emotion 98.3. Wikipedia reports that musicians host some of them, including Lana Del Rey and Morgan Wallen on Back Country Radio, Bad Bunny and RaiNao on Cocoteo FM, and Robyn and Alex Consani on Flash FM. The original score is by the French duo Justice, and players can listen to on-demand podcasts on the in-game phone.

## Platforms

The game is announced for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## Confirmed

- Two playable protagonists
- A single large state to explore, with several distinct regions
- Radio stations, an official score and a soundtrack
- 30 FPS on both consoles at launch

## Unconfirmed

Details such as an online mode, mission structure or an official map size have not been confirmed by Rockstar here, so we do not list them as fact.
