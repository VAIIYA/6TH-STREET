## Overview

Grand Theft Auto VI is an open-world action game set in the state of Leonida. Players follow two protagonists, Jason and Lucia.

## Platforms

The game is announced for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## Confirmed

- Two playable protagonists
- A single large state to explore, with several distinct regions
- Radio stations, an official score and a soundtrack

## Unconfirmed

Details such as online mode, mission structure or map size have not been officially detailed here, so we do not list them as fact.
