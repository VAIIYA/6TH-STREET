## Overview

Jason is one of the two playable protagonists of Grand Theft Auto VI. Rockstar's official description says he and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. He appears in both official trailers.

## Background

According to Wikipedia's summary of the game, Jason worked for local drug runners in the Leonida Keys after serving in the Army. His landlord there is Brian Heder, a long-time drug runner played by Stephen Root.

## The story

When an easy score goes wrong, Jason and Lucia end up in the middle of a criminal conspiracy that stretches across the state of Leonida. They are forced to rely on each other more than ever if they want to make it out alive. Wikipedia describes the trigger as a failed bank heist.

## How he plays

- The game is played as Jason or Lucia. In free roam you can split the two up to do their own thing, or keep them together.
- A Criminal Profile rating tracks each protagonist's reputation. Rockstar says it is not meant to make them do-gooders: it tracks whether crimes stay professional or turn needlessly violent, with consequences that follow.
- Jason can gain or lose weight, and staying awake for days becomes visible on his face.
- Players can customise his hair, clothes and accessories.

## People around him

Wikipedia lists Cal Hampton, Jason's paranoid friend, Raul Bautista, a seasoned bank robber, and Brian Heder among the other characters.

## What is confirmed

- Jason is a playable protagonist alongside Lucia.
- He appears in both official trailers.
