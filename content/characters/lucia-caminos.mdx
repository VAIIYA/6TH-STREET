## Overview

Lucia is one of the two playable protagonists of Grand Theft Auto VI. The first trailer opens with her in a correctional facility, and she is shown throughout both trailers alongside Jason.

## Story

Together with Jason she gets caught up in a criminal conspiracy across Leonida after a seemingly easy job goes wrong.

## What is confirmed

- Lucia is a playable protagonist.
- She is the first female lead in a mainline Grand Theft Auto title.
