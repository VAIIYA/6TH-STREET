## Overview

Lucia is one of the two playable protagonists of Grand Theft Auto VI. The first trailer opens with her in a correctional facility, and she is shown throughout both trailers alongside Jason.

## Background

According to Wikipedia's summary of the game, Lucia was imprisoned at Leonida Penitentiary after fighting for her family from Liberty City. It describes her as the series' first non-optional female protagonist.

## The story

Together with Jason she gets caught up in a criminal conspiracy across Leonida after a seemingly easy job goes wrong. Wikipedia describes the trigger as a failed bank heist, after which the two are forced to protect each other.

## How she plays

- The game is played as Lucia or Jason. In free roam you can split the two up to do their own thing, or keep them together.
- A Criminal Profile rating tracks her reputation based on how professional or needlessly violent her crimes are.
- Lucia can gain or lose weight, and staying awake for days shows on her face.
- Players can customise her hair, clothes and accessories.
- NPCs react to what she does. Wikipedia gives the example of people nervously moving away when a masked Lucia draws a gun before a store robbery.

## What is confirmed

- Lucia is a playable protagonist.
- She is the first female lead in a mainline Grand Theft Auto title.
