## The rumour

Wedbush analyst Michael Pachter, speaking to the BBC, estimated a PC release for Grand Theft Auto VI around mid-2027. The game is currently announced only for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## Status

This is an estimate, not an announcement. Rockstar has not said anything about a PC version.
