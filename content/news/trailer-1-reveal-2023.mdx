## What happened

Rockstar Games released the first trailer for Grand Theft Auto VI on December 4, 2023. A fan-run site reports it had been announced on November 8, 2023.

## Views

The trailer took in about 60 million views in its first 12 hours, according to Video Games Chronicle. Guinness recognised it as the most-viewed game reveal on YouTube in 24 hours, with around 90 million views.
