## What happened

On May 21, 2026 Take-Two Interactive, the parent company of Rockstar, reported its earnings and reaffirmed the November 19, 2026 release date for Grand Theft Auto VI. According to GTA Intel, it also set its forecast for the next fiscal year at $8.0 to $8.2 billion.

## August

Take-Two repeated the date on its August 7, 2026 earnings call.
