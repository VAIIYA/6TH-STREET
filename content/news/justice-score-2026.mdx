## What happened

Rockstar published a Newswire post on October 29, 2026 about an original score for Grand Theft Auto VI by Justice. GTA Boom reports that the score arrives on December 11, 2026, separately from a 34-song Rockstar album due on November 19. We have not seen that date in Rockstar's own post.
