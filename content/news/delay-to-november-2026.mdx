## What happened

On November 6, 2025 Rockstar Games announced that Grand Theft Auto VI would now release on Thursday, November 19, 2026. Game Informer reported the news alongside Take-Two's quarterly earnings.

## Why

Rockstar said the extra time was needed to finish the game with the level of polish it wants to deliver.

## Timeline of dates

- The game was originally projected for fall 2025.
- It was then dated for May 2026.
- It moved to November 19, 2026 with this announcement.

## Platforms

PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
