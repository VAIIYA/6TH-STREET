## Current date

Rockstar Games has announced a release date of November 19, 2026. Always check Rockstar's official channels for the latest information.

## History

- The game was first targeted for 2025.
- It was later dated for May 26, 2026.
- It was then moved to November 19, 2026, announced on November 6, 2025.

## Pre-load

Digital pre-orders can start pre-loading on November 12, 2026. See [pre-orders and editions](/news/pre-orders-and-editions).
