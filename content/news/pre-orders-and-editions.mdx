## What was announced

On June 24, 2026 Rockstar Games announced that pre-orders for Grand Theft Auto VI would open at midnight local time on June 25. The game is a single-player experience for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and is still dated for November 19, 2026. No PC version was mentioned.

## Editions and prices

- **Standard Edition:** $79.99
- **Ultimate Edition:** $99.99, which adds an exclusive collection of premium vehicles, weapons, apparel and action across Jason and Lucia's story.

## Pre-order bonuses

Every pre-order or purchase made before November 20, 2026 includes the Vintage Vice City Pack. Digital pre-orders also receive one free month of GTA+.

## Pre-loading

Digital pre-orders can start pre-loading on November 12, 2026. The physical version contains a download code in the box and is also available from November 12 so that it can be pre-loaded.

## Where to buy

The game is sold through the PlayStation Store, the Microsoft Store, the Rockstar Games Store and other retailers and storefronts.

## Age rating

At the time of the announcement the game was listed as not yet rated. Check Rockstar's official channels for any change since then.
