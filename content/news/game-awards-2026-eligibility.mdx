## What happened

Reports from October 2026 say that Grand Theft Auto VI is eligible for nomination at The Game Awards 2026, including Game of the Year. The show's eligibility rules require a game to be commercially available by November 20, 2026 at 9:00 PM PST.

## Timing

The game launches on November 19, 2026, one day before that cutoff. The ceremony takes place on December 10, 2026. Some outlets note that voters have little time to play it before then.
