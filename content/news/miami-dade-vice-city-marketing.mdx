## What happened

Miami-Dade County announced a GTA VI marketing package with Rockstar on October 29, 2026. Reports put it at about $3.9 to $4 million. The package includes paid advertising at the airport and on the transit system.

## Welcome to Vice City sign

In September 2026 the county approved a "Welcome to Vice City" sign on the county-owned Kaseya Center. Local10 reports that the mayor signed the deal.

## Objections

Several county commissioners said the agreements went ahead without a full commission vote. Sheriff Rosie Cordero-Stutz and Commissioner J.C. Bermudez criticised the deal, partly because of how the game depicts violence against police.
