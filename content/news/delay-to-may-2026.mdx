## What happened

On May 2, 2025 Rockstar Games announced that Grand Theft Auto VI would move from its 2025 window to May 26, 2026. Rockstar apologised for the wait and said the extra time was needed to meet its quality standards.

## What came next

The date changed again in November 2025. See [the delay to November 2026](/news/delay-to-november-2026).
