## Overview

Leonida is the state in which Grand Theft Auto VI takes place. The story of Jason and Lucia stretches across the whole of it, from the neon of Vice City to swamps, islands and farmland.

## Regions

Rockstar has named several regions of the map:

- **Vice City**, inspired by Miami
- **Leonida Keys**, inspired by the Florida Keys
- **Grassrivers**, inspired by the Everglades
- **Port Gellhorn**
- **Ambrosia**
- **Mount Kalaga National Park**

## A Florida on steroids

Wikipedia reports that the world parodies 2020s American culture, with satirical takes on social media, influencer culture, police technology such as body cameras, and internet memes such as Florida Man. Rockstar's writers have said the satire has to be over the top, but that they were careful about what to mock so it would not age quickly.

## Weather

Rockstar re-engineered the weather system from Red Dead Redemption 2 to feel more localised and persistent. Clouds move across the map, Leonida can be hit by seasonal hurricanes and tropical storms, and heavy rain leaves puddles behind. Rainbows can appear under the right lighting.
