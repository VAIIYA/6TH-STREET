## Overview

Leonida is the state in which Grand Theft Auto VI takes place. The story of Jason and Lucia stretches across the whole of it.

## Regions

Rockstar has named several regions of the map:

- Leonida Keys
- Grassrivers
- Port Gellhorn
- Ambrosia
- Mount Kalaga National Park
- Vice City
