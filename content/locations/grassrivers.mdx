## Overview

The Grassrivers is a rural, swampy region of Leonida. Trailer footage shows airboats and wildlife among the marshes.

## Inspiration

The region is inspired by the Everglades, the wetlands of southern Florida, which sets it apart from the urban neon of Vice City.
