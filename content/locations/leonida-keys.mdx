## Overview

The Leonida Keys are one of the named regions of the map. Trailers show boats, beaches and waterfront bars in a tropical setting.

## Inspiration

The region is inspired by the Florida Keys, in the same way that Vice City is inspired by Miami and the Grassrivers by the Everglades.

## In the story

Wikipedia's summary places the early life of Jason Duval here, working for local drug runners after his time in the Army.
