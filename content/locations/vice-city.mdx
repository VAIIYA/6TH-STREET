## Overview

Vice City returns as the centrepiece of Grand Theft Auto VI, set in the fictional state of Leonida. Rockstar calls it "the darkest side of the sunniest place in America".

## Setting

The game is a modern take on the series' Florida-inspired world, with beaches, nightlife, wealth and crime sitting side by side. Wikipedia reports that the world parodies 2020s American culture, including social media and influencer culture.

## How Rockstar built it

- Rockstar began research trips to Miami in 2015, speaking to locals such as law enforcement, club promoters, ex-criminals and gun dealers.
- More than 50 street artists were engaged to design murals for Vice City neighbourhoods.
- Miami-based stylist Jillian Carr has worked with Rockstar since 2023 on clothing that mirrors real fashion trends.

## People of Vice City

Wikipedia names Boobie Ike, who runs a business empire in the city, and Dre'Quan Priest, who owns the record label Only Raw Records with him. The label has signed the musical duo Bae-Luxe and Roxy, who perform as Real Dimez.

## Surroundings

Vice City is only one part of a larger map that includes the Leonida Keys, the Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.
